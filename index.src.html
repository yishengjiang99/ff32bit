Zero-copy

“Zero-copy” here means the file bytes change owner instead of being cloned through the main thread. postMessage normally structured-clones data. A transfer list moves the object: the sender loses it, the receiver gets it.

The worker fetches Range slices of the WAV. It then does one of two transfers into the worklet’s MessagePort:

  • Chrome and Firefox accept a ReadableStream in the transfer list. The worklet reads that same stream.
  • Safari throws DataCloneError on a stream. The worker transfers each chunk’s ArrayBuffer instead. The buffer is detached, not copied.

Decoding is not free. The worklet turns integer PCM into floats and writes those into the Float32Arrays Web Audio already allocated for process(). The network hop is the part that does not copy.

Three threads

Main thread

The tap creates the AudioContext, calls resume(), and wires a worker to an AudioWorkletNode. It never reads the file.
